Signature ritual

The Hyggee Spa

One hour of scalp and hair care, paced so that nothing is asked of you. It is the visit most guests book first, and the one they come back for.

Duration 1 hour Price SAR 490 Reserve this ritual ↗

What it is

Closer to a spa treatment than a salon wash.

A salon washes your hair to prepare it for something else. Here the washing is the service: warm water poured by hand, a slow scalp cleanse, then extended work through the scalp, neck and shoulders. Hair is dried at the end, or left as it is if you prefer.

Nothing about it is clinical. It is a comfort experience, designed around temperature, pressure, light and pace — the four things hygge asks you to get right.

The hour, in order

Six stages, none of them yours to manage.

Timings are approximate — your specialist adjusts to your scalp and to how much quiet you want.

  1. 01

    Arrive 5 min

    Tea, a seat, and enough time to stop moving. Come ten minutes early if you would like to drink it slowly.

  2. 02

    Consult 5 min

    Your specialist asks about your scalp, your hair, how much pressure you like and whether you want to talk or not. Mention allergies and sensitivities here.

  3. 03

    Cleanse 15 min

    Warm water, poured rather than sprayed, twice through. This is the stage guests describe afterwards as the reason they came back, usually without being able to explain why.

  4. 04

    Release 20 min

    Scalp, nape, neck and shoulders worked through by hand. The longest stage, and the one that lengthens further if you add the face or buccal massage.

  5. 05

    Restore 10 min

    Treatment through the hair, a warm towel, and a few minutes where deliberately nothing happens at all.

  6. 06

    Finish 5 min

    Dried and finished by a specialist, or towel-dried and left alone. Water, and an exit no one hurries you through.

Who it suits

  • A first head spa, if you have never had one
  • Anyone who carries tension in the neck and shoulders
  • Guests who want an hour with no decisions in it
  • Two people together, booked side by side

Before you come

  • No preparation. Come with your hair as it usually is
  • Arrive ten minutes early if you would like tea first
  • Say at booking if you would prefer a specific specialist
  • Tell your specialist about allergies or scalp sensitivity

Afterwards

  • Your hair is washed, so leave a little time if you are going out
  • Ask for a blow-dry at booking if you want to leave styled
  • Keep the rest of the evening gentle, if you can
  • Book the next one before you leave — evenings fill first
