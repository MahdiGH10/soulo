Hygge, in Jeddah

Comfort, treated as a craft.

Head & Co. was built around a single idea borrowed from the Danish word hygge: that ease is something you can design. Temperature, light, pressure, pace — each one decided in advance so that nothing during your visit needs deciding by you.

The result is a scalp and hair experience closer to a spa treatment than a salon wash, followed — if you like — by nails, styling and a slow return to the street outside.

The Hyggee Ritual

Six stages, one hour, no decisions.

Every visit follows the same arc. Knowing it in advance is part of the rest.

01 / 06

Read the ritual in full ↗

  1. 01

    Arrive

    Shoes off, tea poured, the noise of King Abdulaziz Road left at the door. Nothing begins until you have sat down.

  2. 02

    Consult

    A short conversation about your scalp, your hair and the pressure you prefer. A question of comfort, not diagnosis.

  3. 03

    Cleanse

    Warm water, poured slowly, twice. The stage most guests remember afterwards without being able to say why.

  4. 04

    Release

    Scalp, neck and shoulders worked through by hand. Add the face or buccal massage and this is where the visit lengthens.

  5. 05

    Restore

    Treatment, warm towel, and a few minutes where nothing at all happens. Deliberately.

  6. 06

    Finish

    Dried and styled by a specialist, or left as it is. Nails can follow in the same visit.
