Your first visit

The questions everyone asks first.

Does my hair get wet?
Yes. The warm-water cleanse is the centre of the ritual. Your hair is dried and finished at the end, or towel-dried and left if you prefer.
What should I wear?
Whatever you arrived in. You stay dressed and lie back — nothing needs changing, and your collar stays dry.
How early should I arrive?
Ten minutes, if you would like to drink your tea slowly. The hour you booked is yours either way.
Do I have to talk?
No. Say so at the consultation and your specialist will keep it quiet. Most guests fall asleep somewhere in the fourth stage.
Can two of us come together?
Yes. Book two appointments at the same time and ask for side-by-side when you arrive.
Where do I park?
Street parking along King Abdulaziz Road, busiest in the early evening. Give yourself a few extra minutes after eight.
Is it only for women?
Ask when you book — this is one to confirm with the team directly.
What if I need to change my booking?
Reschedule or cancel from the confirmation you receive when you book. Earlier notice means someone else can take the slot.
